Data Engineer

We are looking for a Data Engineer to design and maintain reliable data pipelines and data processing systems.

Must-Have Requirements:

* 3+ years of data engineering experience
* Strong Python experience
* Strong SQL experience
* Experience building ETL or ELT pipelines
* Experience with relational databases
* Understanding of data warehousing concepts
* Experience working with large datasets

Nice-to-Have:

* Apache Spark
* Databricks
* AWS
* Azure
* Airflow
* Kafka

Responsibilities:

* Build and maintain scalable data pipelines.
* Develop ETL and ELT processes.
* Transform and validate data.
* Optimize SQL queries and data processing jobs.
* Work with analysts and data scientists.
* Monitor data pipeline reliability and performance.
